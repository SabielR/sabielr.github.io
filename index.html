About Sabiel Rodriguez

Sabiel Rodriguez is a Boston-based operations and go-to-market leader whose work spans public policy, regulatory strategy, and business growth.

He began in legal services, managing more than 170 SSDI and SSI cases for people navigating the federal disability system—work grounded in the practical reality of how public institutions treat the people who depend on them. He later moved into Massachusetts state government, contributing to public oversight and accountability and to the regulatory development of an emerging, closely watched industry.

Ahead of the 2024 presidential election, he served with the body administering elections in DeKalb County, Georgia, conducting research and delivering recommendations on equitable ballot access. He holds a Master of Public Policy from Georgetown University's McCourt School, where he was named a National Urban Fellow, a B.A. in Political Science from Providence College, and a Certificate in Regulatory Affairs for Cannabis Control from Clark University.

Today he works in the private sector, leading operations, market-entry strategy, and regulatory expansion—bringing a regulator's perspective to how organizations evaluate risk, compliance, and market readiness.
